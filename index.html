Kindra's story

Kindra was a knight of the Solar Order. During an attack, she left her gate to open a sealed street and save the people inside. The street was saved, but the gate fell. The Order blamed her and took away her rank.

She now carries a broken gate key made into a sword. She wants to find out why the gate lost power before the attack. She is kind and brave, but finds it hard to leave a fight, even when staying will hurt the people she wants to protect.

Patch notes

Every update installs itself when you open the game.

    Install on Android

    1. 1

      Open this page on your phone and tap Download.

    2. 2

      Open SkillBound.apk from your notifications or Downloads.

    3. 3

      If asked, allow your browser to install unknown apps.

    4. 4

      Tap Install. If Play Protect warns you, tap Install anyway.

    Do I download again for every update?

    No. The game checks for updates when it starts and installs them itself. Big updates ask you to download a new version, and your progress is kept.

    The game won't start or looks broken

    Tap Repair on the login screen. It removes downloaded updates and closes the game; the next start downloads a clean copy.

    Is there an iPhone or PC version?

    Not yet. SkillBound is Android only for now.
